Falling short

Rwanda has nearly halved child stunting in twenty years. The children still being left behind are toddlers, they live in the North and West, and they are born into the poorest homes.

01

Progress, but too slow

Stunting fell from 51% in 2005 to 27% in 2025. Over the last decade it dropped about 1.1 points a year. Reaching the 15% NST2 target by 2029 needs about 3 points a year.

It peaks before age two
